# 🍽️ Zomato Restaurant Analysis – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes a Zomato restaurant dataset using **Microsoft Power BI** to understand restaurant distribution, customer ratings, pricing, and service availability.

The objective was to transform raw restaurant data into meaningful **business insights through interactive data visualization**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The analysis focuses on answering the following business questions:

1. Which cities have the largest number of restaurants?
2. How do average restaurant ratings compare across the Top 10 cities?
3. How does the average cost for two vary across different restaurant rating categories?
4. What percentage of restaurants offer online delivery?
5. What percentage of restaurants offer table booking?
6. How are restaurants distributed across different price ranges?

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Top 10 Cities by Number of Restaurants

Analyzed the number of restaurants across different cities and identified the **Top 10 cities** with the highest restaurant counts.

**Key observation:**

* New Delhi has the highest number of restaurants in the dataset.
* Gurgaon and Noida are also among the cities with a large restaurant presence.

---

### 2. Average Restaurant Rating by Top 10 Cities

Compared the **average restaurant rating** across the Top 10 cities.

This visualization helps understand how customer ratings vary between cities with significant restaurant presence.

---

### 3. Average Cost for Two by Restaurant Rating

Analyzed the relationship between **restaurant rating categories** and the **average cost for two people**.

The analysis shows how pricing varies across different rating categories.

> **Note:** This analysis shows an association between rating categories and average cost. It does not establish that higher ratings cause higher prices.

---

### 4. Online Delivery Availability

Analyzed the percentage of restaurants that provide online delivery.

**Result:**

* Restaurants offering online delivery: **74.34%**
* Restaurants without online delivery: **25.66%**

This provides an overview of how widely online delivery services are available across the restaurants in the dataset.

---

### 5. Table Booking Availability

Analyzed the availability of table booking services.

**Result:**

* Restaurants offering table booking: **12.12%**
* Restaurants without table booking: **87.88%**

This helps understand the adoption of table booking services among restaurants in the dataset.

---

### 6. Restaurant Distribution by Price Range

Analyzed the distribution of restaurants across the available **price range categories**.

This helps understand how restaurants are distributed across different pricing segments.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

* **New Delhi** has the largest number of restaurants in the dataset.
* **74.34%** of restaurants offer online delivery.
* **25.66%** of restaurants do not offer online delivery.
* **12.12%** of restaurants offer table booking.
* **87.88%** of restaurants do not offer table booking.
* Restaurant pricing varies across different rating categories.
* The dataset contains restaurants distributed across multiple price ranges and cities.
